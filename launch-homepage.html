WHAT IT DOES

A careful toolkit for your device storage.

The app is designed around user-authorized Android storage scopes and reversible review workflows.

⌕

Offline scans

Run basic or deep investigations locally across MediaStore, Downloads, and folders you grant.

▦

Evidence review

See source, size, type, and recovery confidence before choosing restore or cleanup.

♧

App-managed bin

◫

Identify duplicate candidates and estimate reclaimable space without automatic deletion.

✦

Premium analysis

Plus and Pro plans unlock deeper scan allowances, removable storage review, and an ad-free experience.

◉

Honest confidence

Results are labelled as visible, trashed, unindexed, duplicate, or low-confidence rather than overpromised.

PRIVACY BOUNDARY

Powerful within Android’s rules.

RecoverScope cannot bypass encryption, access another app’s private sandbox, or read raw internal NAND/UFS/eMMC blocks from a normal Android app. “Deleted” data may be unrecoverable after provider cleanup, discard, garbage collection, or encryption.

That is why every action is explicit, source-labelled, and review-first.
